Delete your data

FreeEQ has no accounts, so there is no account to delete. Your profiles and settings stay on your phone, and you control them.

Last updated 3 November 2026.

Data on your phone

To delete everything FreeEQ stores, use either option:

  • Uninstall FreeEQ.
  • Open your phone’s Settings, go to Apps, then FreeEQ, then Storage, and tap Clear data.

Android removes the data right away. FreeEQ keeps no copy, unless you saved a backup or shared a link yourself. Delete those files or links separately.

Contribution records

If you purchased an in-app contribution, RevenueCat holds an anonymous purchase record and Google keeps your order history. Google and RevenueCat keep some records for as long as tax and accounting rules require.

To ask for your RevenueCat record to be deleted, send your Google Play order number to the developer at github.com/0xrlawrence. Requests are answered within 30 days. Your Google order history is managed in your Google Account.

This website

The website has no accounts and stores nothing about you. The web host’s standard access logs are deleted automatically on its own schedule.
